# Uninstalling

1. Settings > Apps > Installed apps > BayLauncher > **Uninstall** (or Start > BayLauncher > **Uninstall BayLauncher**).
2. Approve the Windows prompt.
3. BayLauncher closes, and the program and its Start menu folder are removed. The Windows account you uninstall from
   starts to the normal desktop again. Any other account that started into the cards gets the normal desktop at its
   next sign-in.
4. The uninstaller asks whether to delete your settings, card pictures and logs. Choose **No** to keep them for a
   later reinstall.
